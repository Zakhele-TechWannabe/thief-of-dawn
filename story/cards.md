# Story cards

Shown full-screen between levels with a typewriter reveal. Each `## key` starts a card; the text below it is
shown as written (blank lines are kept). Keys the game uses: `intro` (before level 1) and `after1` … `after5`
(after each level; the FATE Mark is granted then). Edit freely, no code changes needed.

## intro

The Grand Festival. Music in Dornis, lanterns over Thalor. Then the Library goes dark.

Morrow the Hollow, keeper of the Council's records, walks out with the Lantern of Eterna, and with Sarai of Sarath. "At dawn," he says, "the past forgets itself."

The vault door seals behind him. You are on the wrong side of it.

## after1

FATE MARK OF TRAVERSA
You can now leap off walls: jump while sliding against one.

The vault opens onto the old mine road. A Thalorian scholar's note, pinned to the stone:

"He goes north, through Norra. Traversa guide you."

## after2

FATE MARK OF VITARIA
Survival leaves its mark. Your maximum health grows by one.

Out of the mines, the festival still roars in Dornis. Nobody knows.

Shadows move on the rooftops: Tenebri shades in Morrow's service.

TO BE CONTINUED

## slice_intro

Slateford, Year 320. A cart-road town in the foothills below Norra.

Renn is fifteen, and a thief. Not a hungry orphan, not a misunderstood hero. Today he was caught lifting a second purse he didn't need.

Last year his mother pledged her rope frame, the family's living, against his debts. At first light they send him to the road gangs for the season.

He doesn't intend to be there.
