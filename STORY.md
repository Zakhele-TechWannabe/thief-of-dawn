# Thief of Dawn — a tale of the Umbraverse

Built on the owner's Umbraverse lore (Google Drive: FATE, Races, Humans, Power System).

## Premise

It is the night of the Grand Festival, the yearly celebration of unity first held by the Dorniths. In the
Grand Library of Thalor rests the **Lantern of Eterna**, a relic of the Legacy FATE. As long as it burns, the
Law of Continuity holds: deeds echo, histories are remembered, the Peace Accord that ended the Great War stays
true.

**Morrow the Hollow**, a Tenebri shade who served the Council of Five as its keeper of records, steals the
Lantern during the festival. He means to carry it to the **Nexus of Creation**, where the six FATEs first made
humanity, and snuff it at dawn. With the Law of Continuity unwoven, the past can be rewritten: the Great War
never ends, the Accord never existed, and the Tenebri, long blamed for the war, become its victors.

He takes a hostage to keep the Order of the Shield at bay: **Sarai of Sarath**, the healer chosen to light the
Lantern at the festival's end.

## The hero

**Kael**, a young Valerin (green-skinned, dark-haired, emerald-eyed) and a squire of the Order of the Shield,
the peacekeepers founded in Year 1650. He was on watch in the Library vaults when the Lantern was taken, and
Morrow sealed him below. He has until dawn.

Valerins value discipline and the blade; Kael's FATE is **Bellara** (Conflict). Each trial he survives earns a
**Mark** from one of the FATEs: in play, +1 maximum health, like Prince of Persia's large potions.

## Story cards (shown between levels; short)

**Intro.** *The Grand Festival. Music in Dornis, lanterns over Thalor. Then the Library goes dark. Morrow the
Hollow, keeper of the Council's records, walks out with the Lantern of Eterna, and with Sarai of Sarath. "At
dawn," he says, "the past forgets itself." The vault door seals behind him, and you are on the wrong side of it.*

**After 1.** *The vault opens onto the old mine road. A Thalorian scholar's note, pinned to the stone:
"He goes north, through Norra. Traversa guide you."*

**After 2.** *Out of the mines, the festival still roars in Dornis. Nobody knows. Shadows move on the
rooftops: Tenebri shades in Morrow's service.*

**After 3.** *From the highest roof you see it: Valerion's gate stands open. Someone let them through.*

**After 4.** *Captain Vesh kneels. "He promised the war would end the other way. That my brother would
live." Beyond the wall, the road to the Nexus glows violet.*

**Ending.** *The Lantern flares as the sun rises. Morrow fades into the morning like any shadow. Sarai lights
the flame, and the Festival of Creation ends the way it always has: remembered. Eterna's Law holds. For now.*

## Characters (for art)

- **Kael**: lean Valerin youth, green skin, black hair tied back, emerald eyes; dark-green tunic, leather bracers,
  sash; curved short sword (only after level 1's sword pickup).
- **Tenebri shade (guard)**: spectral, semi-transparent dark-violet humanoid, wispy edges, glowing pale eyes,
  curved blade.
- **Captain Vesh**: armoured Valerin captain, grey steel, green skin, red cloak. (Can be a recolour/variant of the
  guard rig if budget is short.)
- **Morrow the Hollow**: tall Tenebri in tattered scholar's robes, hollow white mask-like face, carries the
  glowing Lantern.
- **Sarai of Sarath**: maroon skin, black hair, white-and-gold healer's robes (story cards only, optional).
